Understand the Air Force O&M budget

Follow funding from the request through execution, see what changed, and read the source explanation behind a figure.

What’s in this dashboard?

Air Force Operations and Maintenance (AF O&M), 1-year appropriation 3400 only. Supplied documents span FY2021–FY2027; year-end execution is available through FY2025.

A fiscal year (FY) runs from October 1 through September 30 and is named for the year it ends. For example, FY2025 ran from October 1, 2024, through September 30, 2025.

What does that scope include—and leave out?

O&M supports day-to-day operations, readiness, maintenance, training, and civilian personnel. This is one part of the Air Force budget; it does not include separate military personnel, procurement, or research appropriations.

“1-year” describes the appropriation’s availability, not the number of years you can compare. Multi-year and X-year funding are excluded. Coverage varies by year and budget area; missing figures are not zero.

Spending plans and individual contracts are not included. The documents may identify adjustment types without explaining the purpose of every action.

What are OP-5 and PBA-19?

They are exhibits within the Budget Justification Book (J-book), not separate reports.

  • PBA-19: appropriation and Budget Activity summaries, including price and net program changes.
  • OP-5: Subactivity Group detail—what operations are financed and the reconciliation of proposed increases and decreases.

The comparison tab uses the summary at AF and BA level and the detailed exhibit at SAG level. Transfers are separated where the detailed source provides them.

How do fiscal years and document dates fit together?

A J-book’s date tells you when it was prepared. An execution report’s “as of” date tells you when its funding snapshot was taken.

For example, the FY2025 J-book is dated February 2024, during FY2024. It compares the FY2024 estimated budget with the FY2025 Budget Request.

For FY2024 year-end Obligations, use the budget execution report as of September 30, 2024. FY2025 year-end Obligations come from the report as of September 30, 2025.

Understand the basics

What are a Budget Activity (BA) and a Subactivity Group (SAG)?

Start with AF O&M overall, choose a BA for a broad mission grouping, then a SAG for a specific activity within it.

  • BA 01: Operating Forces
  • BA 02: Mobilization
  • BA 03: Training and Recruiting
  • BA 04: Administration and Servicewide Activities

For example, BA 03 includes SAG 032A Specialized Skill Training and SAG 032B Flight Training. The SAG dropdown shows only the selected BA’s activities. Use the breadcrumbs to return to the BA or AF overall.

What are the six budget categories?
Budget Request
Funding requested for the fiscal year.
Congressional Action
The change between Budget Request and Enacted funding.
Enacted
Funding provided through enacted appropriations, within the source’s scope.
Reprogramming / Adjustments
The changes from Enacted funding to Updated Budget.
Updated Budget
The funding position after the reported adjustments; execution reports may call it “Net.”
Obligations
Legal commitments of funds—not cash payments or outlays.

Budget Request, Enacted, and Updated Budget are funding positions. Congressional Action and Reprogramming / Adjustments are changes between positions. A calculated change alone does not identify an individual action.

Why can the documents show different figures for the same year?

They can reflect different dates, funding scope, or classifications. A later report may include enacted funding or adjustments that an earlier J-book could not yet show.

The FY2025 J-book uses an FY2024 estimate because full-year enactment was not available when it was prepared. That estimate is kept separate from final FY2024 Enacted funding.

The dashboard preserves each source’s snapshot and flags unresolved differences. Equal and opposite discrepancies do not, by themselves, prove that a transfer occurred.

How are price, program, and transfer changes different?

Price reflects cost factors, such as inflation and pay rates. Program changes reflect changes in requirements or activities; they can include the removal of one-time funding. Transfers move funding between activities or accounts.

A program decrease is not automatically a recurring cut, and a transfer is not automatically a change in the overall Air Force budget.

About the data

This dashboard explores Air Force Operations and Maintenance (AF O&M), using the annual, 1-year 3400 appropriation. It brings together supplied budget documents for FY2021–FY2027 so you can follow one fiscal year, compare years, and explore funding over time.

Three types of documents provide different views of each fiscal year as they become available:

Budget Justification Books (J-books)
Present the Budget Request and explain proposed changes in programs, prices, and transfers. Available for FY2021–FY2027. PBA-19 is the appropriation and Budget Activity summary; OP-5 is the Subactivity Group detail exhibit within the J-book, not a separate report.
DD 1414 — Base for Reprogramming Actions
Reports the funding base after Congressional Action and documents changes at the level shown in the report. Available for FY2021–FY2026.
Budget execution reports (the supplied “1416” files)
Report Updated Budget, Reprogramming / Adjustments, and Obligations as of a stated date. Available here at September 30 for FY2021–FY2025.

Figures link back to their source documents. Each document captures a particular stage and reporting date, so an earlier budget estimate may differ from a later execution report.

BUDGET GUIDE

The six budget categories

1. Budget Request
The funding requested in the President’s Budget for that fiscal year.
2. Congressional Action
The changes between the Budget Request and Enacted funding. When calculated as Enacted minus Budget Request, this is a net difference—not evidence of a particular congressional action.
3. Enacted
The funding provided through enacted appropriations, within the scope and reporting snapshot shown.
4. Reprogramming / Adjustments
The changes between Enacted funding and the Updated Budget. These can include reprogramming, transfers, supplementals, rescissions, and other adjustments, depending on the source. A calculated net change does not establish an individual action or its cause.
5. Updated Budget
The funding position after the adjustments reflected in the selected report. Execution reports may label this “Net.”
6. Obligations
Legal commitments of funds, such as orders or contracts. They show execution of budget authority, not cash payments or outlays.

Budget Request, Enacted, and Updated Budget are funding positions. Congressional Action and Reprogramming / Adjustments are changes between positions. Obligation rate = Obligations ÷ Updated Budget × 100.

About the year-to-year comparison

This comparison uses the later fiscal year’s Budget Justification Book (J-book) to explain the change from its prior-year funding baseline to the new Budget Request. That baseline is the position used when the book was prepared; it may differ from a later year-end execution report. In the FY2025 book, FY2024 is an estimate.

Price changes
Changes in cost factors, such as inflation or pay rates, for the activities being funded.
Program changes
Changes in activities, workload, force structure, or other requirements. They can also include the removal of one-time funding, so a decrease does not automatically mean a cut to a recurring program.
Transfers
Funding moved between activities or accounts. The detailed Subactivity Group (SAG) reconciliation shows transfers separately when the source does.

Within the J-book, PBA-19 is the appropriation and Budget Activity summary. OP-5 is the Subactivity Group detail exhibit. The summary reports price and net program changes; the detailed exhibit separates transfers where available.

About Funding Over Time

Compare the same funding measure across selected years. Budget shows three positions: Budget Request, Enacted, and Updated Budget.

Reporting basis
Each figure retains its source and reporting date. J-books describe requests; DD 1414 reports the funding base after congressional action; execution reports show later adjustments and Obligations.
Available years
Requests extend through FY2027; year-end execution through FY2025. Missing figures are not zero. Obligations are shown as of September 30.
Comparing dollars
Amounts are nominal dollars—no inflation adjustment. FY2021 includes base and Overseas Contingency Operations (OCO) funding; former OCO costs are incorporated into base in later years. Separately reported mandatory funding is excluded.
