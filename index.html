About

The Air Force O&M Budget Assistant is an independent educational ChatGPT plugin created by Kara Taylor. It is designed for beginners learning to read U.S. Air Force Operations and Maintenance (O&M) budget documents, primarily FY2025 with selected FY2026 material.

It can explain terminology, interpret the packaged source documents, and trace funding from the budget request through congressional action, later adjustments, and obligations when the sources support that trace. It keeps appropriation, Budget Activity, and Subactivity Group levels separate and identifies missing, conflicting, or unreconciled information rather than inventing an answer.

It is not an official Department of the Air Force or Department of Defense product and should not be used as the sole basis for funding, legal, accounting, contracting, or policy decisions.

Budget Framework

Why this framework? Air Force budget reports use different labels for related stages of the funding process. These six categories make the reports easier to compare, while the assistant still preserves and explains the exact terminology used in each source document.

01

Requested budget

The funding proposal submitted for congressional consideration.

02

Congressional adjustments

Changes made through congressional action, including marks, statutory adjustments, and General Provisions, using the source document’s exact terminology when relevant.

03

Enacted or appropriated budget

The enacted or appropriated funding position established by the applicable source.

04

Reprogramming and other adjustments

Later funding changes such as reprogramming actions and other source-documented adjustments. When a source uses a specific term such as internal realignment, the assistant preserves that term.

05

Updated budget / Net

The updated funding position after applicable adjustments reflected in the source documents.

06

Obligations

Legal commitments of funds. Obligations show execution of budget authority, but they are not disbursements or cash payments.

Source terminology still matters

The six categories are a comparison and teaching framework, not replacements for official report labels. When a source uses a more specific term—such as Adjustments Requested by Statute, General Provisions, Above Threshold Reprogramming, Below Threshold Reprogramming, supplemental appropriation, internal realignment, or Net Authority—the assistant should preserve and explain that exact term when it matters.
