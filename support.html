Support & Feedback

If you find an incorrect number, citation problem, confusing explanation, or another issue with the Air Force O&M Budget Assistant, you can report it through the project’s GitHub Issues page.

Please do not submit classified, controlled, sensitive, proprietary, or personal information. GitHub Issues are public. Do not include information you would not want publicly visible.

Submit feedback on GitHub

Useful details to include

Tell us what you were trying to do, what answer or result you received, what seems wrong or could be improved, and which source document was involved if you know it.

This support page is for feedback about the Air Force O&M Budget Assistant. It is not an official Department of the Air Force or Department of Defense support channel.
